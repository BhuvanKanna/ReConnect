# Exercise catalog

Exercises from home and clinic stroke-rehab systems that have been tested with stroke survivors, and the
Mend activity that trains the same movement. Compiled October 2026 from public sources. Some vendors publish
only part of their catalog; that's noted where it applies.

## mRehab (smartphone + 3D-printed household objects, University at Buffalo)

Evidence: 18 people with chronic stroke used it in a 6-week home program; upper-limb mobility improved.
The phone measured repetitions, duration and smoothness (normalized jerk score, zero-crossing rate).
Full list of 12 activities:

| Activity | What the patient does | Mend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vertical bowl | Two-handed lift of a bowl onto a box and back | Lantern Lift |
| Horizontal bowl | Two-handed slide of a bowl across a panel | – |
| Vertical mug | Lift a mug onto a box and back | Lantern Lift |
| Horizontal mug | Move a mug side to side across a panel | – |
| Sip from mug | Bring the mug to the mouth and hold | Lantern Lift |
| Quick twist mug | Turn the hand outward fast and return upright | Pour the Tea |
| Slow pour | Pour slowly; the app warns when pouring too fast | Pour the Tea |
| Walk with mug | Walk 10 s keeping the mug level | – |
| Unlock with key | Turn a key clockwise, then counterclockwise | Combination Lock |
| Turn doorknob | Turn a knob clockwise, then counterclockwise | Combination Lock |
| Phone number | Type a shown number quickly and accurately | – |
| Quick tap | Tap circles as fast as possible with several fingers | – |

Sources: [activity table](https://jneuroengrehab.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12984-019-0592-y/tables/1),
[home study](https://mhealth.jmir.org/article/export/end/mhealth_v8i7e19582)

## Overall evidence for game-based rehab

The 2025 Cochrane review (190 trials, 7,188 participants) found virtual reality and interactive gaming slightly
better than other therapy for arm function, balance and activity limitation, and moderately better for arm
function when added to usual care to increase total practice time.
Source: [Cochrane summary](https://search.pedro.org.au/search-results/record-detail/30080)
