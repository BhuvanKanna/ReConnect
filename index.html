Where these exercises come from

Every exercise comes from a home or clinic rehab program tested with stroke survivors. The right column shows which ReConnect exercise trains the same movement. Some vendors publish only part of their catalog; that's noted where it applies.

The 2025 Cochrane review of 190 trials found game-based and virtual-reality rehab slightly better than other therapy for arm function and balance, with larger gains when it adds practice time on top of usual care. ReConnect is built to add that practice time at home.
