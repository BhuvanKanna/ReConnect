What the tested systems ask patients to do

Every exercise below comes from a home or clinic rehab system that has been tested with stroke survivors. The right column shows which ReConnect activity trains the same movement. Some vendors publish only part of their catalog, noted where it applies.

The 2025 Cochrane review of 190 trials found game-based and virtual-reality rehab slightly better than other therapy for arm function and balance, with larger gains when it adds practice time on top of usual care. ReConnect is built to add that practice time at home.
